Papers, decoded

Papers and essays I'm learning from, rebuilt as interactive guides. Each one explains the main idea and its vocabulary in plain words, with things to click and drag, so no background is needed.

  1. The math inside attention

    A follow-up to The Illustrated Transformer, using the equations from "Attention Is All You Need" (2017)

    How every vector in a Transformer is computed and why: dot products, matrix multiplication, queries and keys, softmax, √dₖ, positional clocks, layer norm and learning by gradient descent, all on a three-word example.

    Dot product, matrix multiplication, transpose, softmax, standard deviation, sine and cosine, ReLU, cross-entropy, gradient

  2. The Illustrated Transformer

    Jay Alammar, 2018, explaining "Attention Is All You Need" (2017)

    How a Transformer reads a sentence: each word looks at the others to understand itself, then the model writes a translation one word at a time.

    Self-attention, query, key, value, softmax, multi-head attention, positional encoding, masking, beam search

  3. Making Deep Learning Go Brrrr From First Principles

    Horace He, 2022

    Why your GPU is slow, and how to tell which of three things is to blame: doing math, moving data, or the software around it.

    Compute-bound, memory-bound, overhead, operator fusion, compute intensity, CUDA Graphs
